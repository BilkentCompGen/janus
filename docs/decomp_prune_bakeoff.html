The two levers & why hybrids win

Round 1 isolated what moves the needle.

Branch-stop minimal anchoring (walk shared backbone to the nearest junction, halt) gives tight, de-tangled footprints at a fraction of a fixed 10-hop ball. Coverage/edge denoise drops shallow error connectors. The full graph "recovers" complex events only as tangle-smears (recip→0); cutting the smearing bridges turns 34–37 of them into clean, tight pieces → recall rises above the ceiling.

What is NOT the lever (measured)

Two sweeps on the branch-stop hybrid — both FLAT on recall.

recip70 = 398 across every anchor radius (2–14) and every denoise threshold (0–12). The 398 is structural to the de-tangling family, not a tuned knob. Denoise/radius only trade a little size & SNP (SNP 19336→19292 as denoise hardens). Only the watershed hub-cut (cutting shared repeat-hub cores, deg≥6) pushed 398→401.

Winner spotlight — hyb_maxrecip (1.44%, recall 401)

Three stacked mechanisms, all reference-free, ~11 s on 43.3M cores:

  1. Seed the somatic signal — every connected tumor-only/normal-only core + both endpoints of every somatic edge (DUP/INV back-edges), shallow error edges gated out.
  2. Branch-stop anchoring — bracket each piece with shared backbone only out to the nearest junction (halt, never cross). Tight footprints → high reciprocal; 2·hops auto-cuts all-shared bridges >8 cores while protecting within-event gaps.
  3. Watershed hub-cut — label each somatic event's territory; cut shared repeat-hub cores (deg≥6) where two events collide, splitting smeared pairs into two tight pieces (the +3 over the other hybrids).

Trade-off it accepts: cover≥.70 dips to 1199 (−37 loose, all tangle-smears that were never clean) and SNP to 19257 (−188; largely a footprint-window effect — the bubbles survive, their tight footprint just falls outside the ±150 bp SNP window). If SNP retention is paramount, hyb_minfull (1.66%, recall 398, SNP 19335, purity 99.8%) or the no-denoise sweep point (T=0: 1.78%, 398, SNP 19336) trade a hair of size for it.

Recommendation

Ship hyb_maxrecip or hyb_balanced as the decomposition substrate for typing. Both: ~1.5% of the graph (≈70× smaller), more cleanly-recoverable SVs than the full graph (398–401 vs 364), ~99.5% somatic-fragment purity, and the highest clean-piece count (175 vs 144) — i.e. more SVs land in a ≤48-node piece the exact canonical-form typer can handle. The residual complex/multi-block events (the ~1000 that are tangle-smears even in the full graph, recip≈0) are the genuine open problem for typing, unchanged by pruning. SNP/indel signal is retained at ≥97% (bubbles survive; the small dip is a footprint-window artifact, not deletion).
