Are the fragments signal or noise?

Two things you asked: what a 2-node graph even is, and how much of the dust is noise.

A 2-node graph = 2 cores + 1 edge, cut loose

When the backbone/anchors around a kept pair are pruned away, the pair survives as its own tiny island. In ② there are 16,901 — mostly low-coverage (median 8 reads vs the 45-read backbone), i.e. shallow somatic spurs or stub remnants. Colour of the pair:
