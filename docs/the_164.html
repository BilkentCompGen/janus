The 164 are all large SVs. The gauntlet splits the truth set at 50bp — SNPs and indels are a separate 25,000-record bucket — so nothing here is a small variant. You were right that they should not vanish by magic. They don't: 59% of them sit where my ruler has no markings.

1 · The refpos gap — the real cause

refpos applies the same (A+X)→(X+B) repeat merge as the graph (refpos.cpp: “byte-identical to fasta_cores … ref_flags + ref_merge”). In a repeat run the cores are absorbed into the flanks, so no core carries a position there.

4.74% of the genome has no refpos core at all — and the distribution is not random:

contig1kb bins with no core

Y, 9, 16, 15, 22, 13 — the satellite and acrocentric chromosomes. This is the repeat merge, not assembly gaps: 0 of the 97 gap-misses are N-runs; all 97 are real sequence.

2 · It is not the filters — every one was rebuilt and measured

Four full rebuilds: mm=1 (441M nodes), vote off (880M), vote+blacklist off (850M). Total cost of the entire pipeline: under 10 records of 164.

stagecostevidence

The “no filter at all” graph (850M nodes, 20× the production graph) buys nothing real and destroys the metric: GAIN fires on 37/37 random loci and no piece is typeable (clean=0).

3 · Length — they are not short

The short-span theory is dead: median length of a miss is 5,746bp vs 7,923bp for a hit. Only 37/164 are under 500bp; 87 are ≥5kb.

The gap-misses are the long ones (median 6,919bp, 59 of them ≥5kb) — big SVs landing in satellite repeats. The genuinely-absent ones are shorter (median 2,362bp).

4 · The 57 that are really missing

Cores present at the breakpoint, metric able to see, signal still not there. This is the only part that is a caller question — 4.1% of the truth set.

reasonn
  • COPY ratio below 1.4 (19) — the duplication is there but its depth ratio does not reach threshold. Worth one look: are these subclonal, or in repeat-confounded depth?
  • JUNC disjoint (16) — novel material at both breakpoints but in different components: the junction bridge is broken in the middle. Almost all delINVdup. This is the one genuine structural failure and the best lead.
  • INVSIG no strand-1 (10) — inversions with no reverse-complement signal. Consistent with the information-theoretic limit: a read entirely inside a large inversion is byte-identical to the reference.
  • no novel at target (8) — the paste site has no novel material.

What I would do

  • Do not touch the pipeline. refine, mm=3, the vote and the blacklist together cost <10 of the 164, and two of them are net positive. Rebuilding without them makes things strictly worse.
  • The ceiling is a property of the ruler, not the caller. 1252/1407 is the best measurable number. 97 records sit in repeat regions where the merge leaves no coordinates — the caller may well be finding them; I cannot currently tell, and I will not claim either way.
  • To actually measure the 97, refpos must be rebuilt so repeat loci keep their coordinates (emit all loci per signature, with a multi-locus resolution rule). That is a change to the diagnostic tool, not to svp. It is the single highest-value next step.
  • The one real lead is JUNC-disjoint (16, mostly delINVdup): novel material at both ends that fails to connect. That is a genuine graph-structure question worth chasing.
  • Honest residual: ~57 records (4.1%) are genuinely not recoverable from this graph, of which ~10 are the information-theoretic large-inversion case.
